What is an indoor digital twin?

1

Capture

Ordinary overlapping photographs (or a phone video) of a real indoor space.

2

Reconstruct

Camera poses, dense geometry, a surface mesh and photo texture: a faithful 3D copy.

3

Explore

Walk through it freely in any web browser. No headset, no app install.
