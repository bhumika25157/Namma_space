About this reconstruction

Pipeline (open-source, CPU-only)

  1. Capture – one ordinary handheld smartphone video (no LiDAR, depth sensor or IMU data used).
  2. Frame selection – ffmpeg blurdetect; sharpest frame per window kept.
  3. Structure-from-Motion – COLMAP 4.2: SIFT features, sequential + loop-closure matching, incremental mapping → camera poses + sparse cloud.
  4. Gravity alignment – COLMAP orientation aligner (upright-camera prior).
  5. Multi-View Stereo – OpenMVS 2.4: depth-map estimation & fusion → dense point cloud.
  6. Surface + texture – OpenMVS Delaunay/graph-cut meshing, view-selected texture atlas → GLB.
  7. Web delivery – glTF-Transform (meshopt geometry, WebP textures) → three.js viewer with BVH-accelerated collision.
